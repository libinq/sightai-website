## Background

A tier-1 supplier producing stamped body parts on multiple lines running three shifts.

## Challenge

- Manual inspection drifted with shifts and fatigue, letting defects escape downstream.
- Frequent changeovers meant retuning rule-based vision every time.
- NG parts were sorted by hand, with little traceability.

## Solution

- **Industrial Quality Inspection** — industrial cameras and lighting at each station, with millisecond edge decisions.
- Reject signals sent to the PLC over Profinet; decisions and images written back to MES.
- Hard cases fed back into the **SightAI Platform** for continuous training.
