## Background

A production team focused on outdoor and extreme-sports content, shooting each session on several action cameras and drones.

## Challenge

- Head- and chest-mounted footage was too shaky to use.
- Cameras didn't match, so colorists matched shot by shot.
- Release schedules were tight.

## Solution

- **FPV Stabilization** — whole sessions processed in batch, with smoothing tuned per shot.
- **Color & Enhancement** — every camera matched to the main angle, with LUTs exported for reuse.
