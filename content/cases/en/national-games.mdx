## Background

The National Games are China's largest multi-sport event, spanning several cities, many venues and heavy crowd movement.

## Challenge

- Venues, final construction zones and crowded areas put heavy pressure on safety teams.
- There are far more camera feeds than security staff can watch.
- Visitors and staff ask the same wayfinding and schedule questions all day.

## Solution

- **Safety Behavior Recognition** — existing venue cameras connected for real-time alerts on restricted-area intrusion, climbing, smoke and fire; inference stays on site.
- **AI Digital Human** — service-point presenters answering wayfinding and event questions in several languages.
