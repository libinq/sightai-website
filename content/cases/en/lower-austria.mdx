## Background

Lower Austria is Austria's largest federal state by area. The state government plans and maintains its road network across towns, countryside and mountain terrain.

## Challenge

- Manual surveys were slow and infrequent, so defects were found late.
- Different inspectors rated defects differently, making segments hard to compare.
- Survey notes had to be turned into maintenance work orders by hand.

## Solution

- **Road Defect Detection** — cameras and edge boxes on regular patrol vehicles capture the road surface at traffic speed; cracks, potholes and rutting are detected and graded automatically.
- **SightAI Platform** — grading thresholds configured to local maintenance standards, with results mapped per segment and exported to the maintenance system.
