## Background

FIFA is the world governing body of football and organizes international tournaments including the FIFA World Cup. During a tournament it must keep a global, multilingual audience supplied with news and behind-the-scenes content.

## Challenge

- Every update needs several language versions, faster than human presenters and voice talent can be scheduled.
- Footage from different venues and cameras arrives with inconsistent color.
- Event content is time-critical, so the production chain has to be as automated as possible.

## Solution

- **AI Digital Human** — a custom presenter and voice generate multilingual news videos from the match schedule.
- **Color & Enhancement** — every camera matched to a reference, with exposure and noise fixes for night games.
