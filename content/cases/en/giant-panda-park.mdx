## Background

Giant Panda National Park was formally established in 2021 and spans Sichuan, Shaanxi and Gansu provinces. It protects the giant panda and many other rare species across rugged, remote terrain.

## Challenge

- The forest area is too large for patrols to spot fires or illegal entry in time.
- Most monitoring sites sit on weak links, so live video can't be streamed back.
- Night and infrared footage is noisy and low on detail, limiting later analysis.

## Solution

- **Safety Behavior Recognition** — edge boxes at monitoring sites detect smoke, fire and perimeter intrusion locally, sending back only alerts and snapshots.
- **Color & Enhancement** — low-light enhancement and denoise for night and infrared footage.
