## Background

The operator of a large petrochemical park whose process units, tank farms and loading areas see constant movement of staff and contractors.

## Challenge

- The control room watched hundreds of feeds and missed violations.
- Smoke and open flame must be caught early in process areas.
- Video had to stay on the park's internal network.

## Solution

- **Safety Behavior Recognition** — edge boxes on existing cameras detect missing PPE, zone intrusion, smoking, smoke and fire.
- Alerts pushed via Webhook to the park safety platform and site leads.
- On-prem deployment; video never leaves the park.
