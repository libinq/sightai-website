## Background

The Asian Football Confederation governs football in Asia. With 47 member associations, it runs competitions such as the AFC Asian Cup and the AFC Champions League for audiences across many languages.

## Challenge

- Large volumes of historic match footage are low-resolution and faded, unfit for HD and digital platforms.
- Each language market needs its own content, which takes time.
- The content team wants more output without more headcount.

## Solution

- **Color & Enhancement** — super-resolution, denoise and color restoration for archive footage.
- **AI Digital Human** — one consistent presenter delivering match recaps and news in several languages.
