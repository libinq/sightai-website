## Background

The China Open is an ATP and WTA tour event held in Beijing. During the tournament it supplies social channels and broadcast partners with a steady stream of on-site content.

## Challenge

- Player-view, ball-kid and handheld action-camera footage is visibly shaky.
- Day and night sessions differ sharply in lighting, and cameras don't match.
- Social content has to go out minutes after a match ends.

## Solution

- **FPV Stabilization** — trajectory smoothing and horizon leveling that keep the on-court energy.
- **Color & Enhancement** — low-light enhancement for night sessions and multi-camera matching for one consistent look.
